About The Chapter

Built in Spring Lake. Guided by service.

The Alpha Alpha Gamma Sigma Alumni Chapter continues Phi Beta Sigma’s legacy through brotherhood, education, and social action.

History

Chartered for local impact.

The Alpha Alpha Gamma Sigma Chapter received its charter on September 9, 2010, in Spring Lake, North Carolina.

Chartering members include Hon. Brother Chris V. Rey, Bro. JerMaine Kearney, the late Bro. Dr. Paul E. Jones, Bro. Durwin Boone, and Bro. Lawrence Smalls.

The chapter was formed to answer local community needs and extend the fraternity’s commitment to service, scholarship, and civic leadership.

Chapter Focus

  • Mentorship and youth development
  • Educational advancement and scholarships
  • Civic partnerships and neighborhood service
  • Values-based brotherhood and leadership

Brotherhood

A network of men committed to service, accountability, growth, and community care.

Scholarship

Support for education, student achievement, and lifelong learning.

Social Action

Programs and advocacy that strengthen families, schools, and neighborhoods.
