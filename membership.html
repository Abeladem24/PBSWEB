Membership

Values-based brotherhood.

Membership in Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity, Inc. is by invitation only and is grounded in character, service, and personal development.

Application Materials

Prepare with clarity.

  • Completed membership application
  • College degree or official transcript
  • Hold harmless form
  • Three recommendation letters, including one from a financial brother
  • Personal statement of interest
  • Required membership fees and assessments

Anti-Hazing Commitment

The membership process does not include hazing. Training is conducted in a classroom setting, and hazing in any form is prohibited by fraternity policy.
